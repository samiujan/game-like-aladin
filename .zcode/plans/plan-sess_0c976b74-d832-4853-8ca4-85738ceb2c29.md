# Silk & Steel — Sega-Aladdin-style action platformer

A 16-bit side-scrolling action game in the spirit of Disney's *Aladdin* (Sega Genesis): fluid sword combat, throwable knives, bouncy acrobatics — set in a Silk Road village in Central Asia, 1500s. Riders of a renegade warlord are raiding your village; you chase them through orchard, bazaar, and mountain gate to take them back.

## Tech
- **Vanilla JS + Canvas 2D**, ES modules, zero dependencies, no build step. Open `index.html` and play.
- Internal resolution **320×180**, integer-scaled ×4 (1280×720), `image-rendering: pixelated`.
- Fixed 60 Hz timestep loop, AABB physics vs a 16px tile grid, camera with lookahead + smooth follow.
- Levels authored as **ASCII string maps** (readable, easy to tweak).

## Art — procedural pixel sprites
- String-based pixel maps + letter palette, baked to offscreen canvases at boot. Warm Genesis-era palette (adobe, terracotta, indigo, apricot).
- Hero (~16×24px): run (6 frames), jump, crouch, ledge-climb, 3-slash combo, throw, hurt, idle.
- Parallax layers painted per level: sky gradient, mountains, minaret/poplar skyline, adobe houses, foreground props — with per-level time-of-day palettes (morning / noon / dusk-night).
- Juice: hit-stop on sword connect, screen shake, white hit-flash, dust, sparks, coin-poof deaths, floating score text.

## Gameplay
- **Movement:** run, variable-height jump (coyote time + jump buffering), crouch, ledge grab & climb, bounce off market awnings.
- **Sword:** 3-hit combo (slash, slash, rising finisher) with a small lunge. **Deflect:** slashing at the right moment knocks enemy knives back — key to the fun.
- **Throwing knives:** limited ammo (start 5, cap 9), slight gravity arc, resupplied from smashed pots/crates and dropped pickups.
- **Health:** 4 hearts, invulnerability blink after hits; 3 lives; carved banner-post checkpoints.
- **Pickups:** coins (score), dried apricots (heal), knife bundles, hidden treasure chests.

## Enemies & boss
1. **Sword bandit** — advances, slashes; sometimes raises guard (blocks front attacks — jump behind him).
2. **Knife thrower** — keeps distance, lobs knives, retreats when pressed.
3. **Spear charger** — telegraphs, then charges the whole lane; jump over him.
4. **Brute (mini-boss, bazaar)** — armored, big club, blocks knives; punish after his swing.
5. **Boss: the Warlord of the Iron Gate** — big-sword lunges and jump-slams (shockwave to jump); phase 2 below half HP adds knife fans and speed. Ornate HP bar.

## Levels
1. **Orchard Outskirts** (golden morning) — apricot orchards, fields, first yurts; teaches all moves gently.
2. **Bazaar of the Red Domes** (noon) — stalls, awning bounce-pads, rooftops, caravanserai courtyard, brute fight.
3. **Pass of the Iron Gate** (dusk → night) — cliffs, caltrop traps, fortress walls, boss arena.

## Audio (all WebAudio-synthesized, no files)
SFX: slash whoosh, metallic clang, throw, coin blips, hits, boss sting. Music: small loop sequencer — drone + plucked minor-pentatonic melodies + hand-drum, per-level variant + boss track. Mute with M.

## Controls
Arrows/WASD move · Space/Z jump · X sword · C throw knife · ↓ crouch · ↑ climb ledge · Enter start/pause.
